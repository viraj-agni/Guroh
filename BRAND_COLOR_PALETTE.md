# Project Vidya — Official Brand Guidelines & Logo Specification

## 1. Official Brand Mark & Emblem Story

The official Project Vidya mark is an iconic, geometric dual-meaning emblem:
1. **The 3-Step Staircase:** Directly encodes our core pedagogical flywheel (**Stage 1: Intuition → Stage 2: Experimentation → Stage 3: Mastery**).
2. **The Stylized 'G' Structural Curve:** A subtle, modern structural nod to our lineage (**Guroh / Agni Labs**) while avoiding generic corporate lettermarks.

```
         ┌───┐
      ┌──┘   └──┐
   ┌──┘         │
   │  ┌──────┐  │   <-- 3-Step Learning Flywheel forming 'G'
   └──┴──────┴──┘
```

## 2. Color Specification & Matrix

### Primary Background Container: Deep Crimson / Dark Oxblood Gradient
Selected for Project Vidya's primary logo container, hero brand elements, and institutional identity. Communicates academic rigor, authority, and focused passion.

```yaml
Color Tokens:
  Primary_Background: "#8D1516" (Deep Crimson / Dark Oxblood)
  Gradient_Vignette_Dark: "#4A0B0C" (Dark Vignette Edge)
  Foreground_Mark: "#F2EFEB" (Warm Crisp Off-White / Silver)
  Accent_Gold: "#A37134" (Warm Gold)

Color Standards:
  HEX: "#8D1516"
  RGB: "rgb(141, 21, 22)"
  CMYK: "cmyk(25%, 100%, 98%, 34%)"
```

## 4. Usage Guidelines for Engineering & Product (Shuri & Tony)

* **App Icon / Favicon:** 512x512px rounded container (squircle) with `#8D1516` gradient background and centered off-white `#F2EFEB` emblem mark.
* **UI Navigation Header:** Render the white/silver emblem at 32x32px or 40x40px on top dark headers or in clean monochrome for light headers.
* **Product Alignment:** Maintain the 3-step staircase narrative across marketing materials, onboarding flows, and investor decks.
